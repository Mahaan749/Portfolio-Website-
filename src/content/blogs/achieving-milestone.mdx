# The Blog Is Online. Nothing Exploded.

The blog is finally online. The page loads, the links work, and no alarms have gone off. In cybersecurity terms, that counts as a calm and successful deployment.

## Why this exists

I wanted a place to document what I learn from labs, coursework, security tools, and the occasional problem that looked easy until I opened the terminal.

## What will be here

- **Lab notes:** what I tested, what happened, and what I would change next time.
- **Defensive security:** monitoring, triage, logs, networking, and incident-handling fundamentals.
- **Learning updates:** useful ideas from coursework and training, minus the dramatic motivational speech.
- **Mistakes:** because a command that fails three times is technically a learning sequence.

## Ground rules

Everything here is for learning and authorised practice. No stolen credentials, no mystery databases, and definitely no production system being used as a lab because “it will probably be fine.”

## First entry complete

That is enough ceremony for one blog launch. Now I can return to the traditional cybersecurity workflow: inspect logs, take notes, and wonder why DNS is involved again.
